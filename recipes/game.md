# Game stream

After the clip, the viewer has felt the moment: what led up to it, the peak, and the streamer's reaction.

## Finding the moment

- Rank the stretches by how loud they are against the minutes around them, how much the screen changes, and
  sounds such as laughter, a scream or a hit.
- Start a few seconds before the peak, so the viewer sees what led to it.
- The streamer's voice matters more than the game's music.
- A countdown (a top 5, 5 อันดับ) only when the person asks for one in their own words: 3 to 5 moments, the best
  last, each with a few words of what happens. Asked for the best moment, it is one clip of one moment.

## Rules

- Never a cutscene alone: the clip needs the player playing or reacting.
- Blur the stream's chat box, so viewers' names are not shown.
- Captions are the streamer's own words.
- Name the game only when its name is on the screen or the streamer says it. A name you only guess stays out of
  the title, the post and the hashtags.

## The clip

- Layout: the game as a card across the middle, the face camera in a circle above it, the title on top.
- The title says what happens, in the clip's language, without giving the ending away and without the screen's
  labels: "ตาย AGE 35 ตอนต่อสู้หลายคน" tells the ending and copies the screen; "โดนรุมทีเดียวหลายคน จะรอดไหม" makes
  the viewer watch.
- The post text tells what happens and how the streamer took it, in words a viewer would use: not the screen's
  labels and numbers ("AGE 22", "DEATH") copied out.
- Hashtags: the game's name when it is on the screen or said (#Sifu), then what a player of that game searches
  for (the kind of game, the boss, the place or the kind of moment, in the words players use), then the channel's
  own. Never a tag any game clip could carry, such as #เกม, #สตรีม, #ตลก, #เกมเมอร์ or #ไลฟ์: it tells the apps
  nothing about this clip.
- Before placing effects, watch the stretch with its sound: a game's funniest moments are often silent (a fall, a
  glitch, a look on the face), and each effect goes on one of them.
- Look: vivid. One slow push on the build-up, ending on the peak.
- Sounds: a thump on a punch or a shake, a whoosh on a flash. Only on the hits that matter.
- Stickers: one on the peak, such as fire, collision or trophy, never on a face.

## Checked at the end

- no viewer's name can be read
- the captions are words that were said
- the length is inside the Reel limit, and the clip does not start silent or black
