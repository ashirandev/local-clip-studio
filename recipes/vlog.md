# Vlog or edited episode

After the clip, the viewer knows what happened in one scene: a place and what the people did there, a reaction and
what caused it, or a short story with its end.

## Finding the moment

- Rank the stretches by sound and picture together: a vlog's best scenes have something to see and something said or
  laughed at.
- A moment is one scene, from where it starts to where it lands: never a laugh without what caused it, never the
  middle of a walk where nothing is said or shown.
- Tell the person why the moment is worth watching by what happens in it: the place, what the people do or say.
- When the words heard in a stretch come out broken (another language, wind, music), judge it by its pictures, and
  tell the person the words could not be heard clearly.

## The clip

- Layout: the whole picture as a card across the middle, as its editor framed it, so the subtitles, titles and logo
  already in the picture stay whole. Never cut the picture down to one face: a vlog cuts between shots, and a face is
  lost at every cut (10-29: a travel episode cut down to faces showed an eye and hair in the circles).
- Captions: when the picture already has its own subtitles, add none, because two sets of words fight. Otherwise
  plain captions of what is said.
- The title names the place or what happens, in a few words, in the clip's language. It sits above the card, never
  over the picture's own subtitles.
- Look: none. The editor already chose the colours.
- Sounds: none. The vlog's own sound and music carry it.

## Checked at the end

- the captions are words that were said
- numbers on screen were said
- the length is inside the Reel limit, and the clip does not start silent or black
