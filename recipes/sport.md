# Sports match

After the clip, the viewer has seen each goal (or point) whole: how the attack was built, the shot, the ball going
in and the celebration, and knows what it did to the score.

## Finding the moment

- The commentator says the goals: listen for the word goal, a name shouted or the new score, then look there.
- A goal is when the score on the screen changes. Read the score at the start and at the end of a stretch: a loud
  stretch where the score stays the same is a near miss, a foul or a save, and comes after the goals.
- The crowd rises only for its own side, so a goal against it is quiet and the loud stretches find only some of the
  goals. Look across the whole match, not only where it is loud, and read the score in each look: when it differs
  between two looks, a goal lies between them, so look there until you see it.
- Rank the stretches by the crowd and the commentator getting louder against the minutes around them.
- Start where the attack starts: the pass, the run or the steal that leads to the shot, often 8 to 15 s before the
  ball goes in. Never start on the shot.
- End after the celebration, before the replay of the same goal and before the kick-off that follows: a replay
  shows again what the viewer has just seen.
- A goal that changes the match (an equaliser, the winner, a late goal) comes before an early one, and a long shot
  or a run past several players before a tap-in.
- Each goal is its own moment. Tell the person how many goals the match has and when each one comes.

## Rules

- The score and the match minute in the title or the post are the ones on the screen at that moment, never worked
  out or guessed.
- Name a team or a player only when the name is on the screen or said.
- Captions are the player's own words when they talk over the match. The commentator is part of the match's sound:
  leave the commentary out of the captions unless the person asks for it.

## The clip

- One clip holds all the goals: propose every goal, then join them into one clip, in match order, not ranked. The
  words over each piece: what happened and the score after it, as "วอลเลย์เข้าไกล 2-0". Give each piece the start where the
  attack starts and the end when the celebration ends, at most 20 s, so all of them fit. Asked for one goal, make one.
- Layout: the whole pitch as a card. The ball and the players move across the whole width, so the sides are never
  cut. When there is a face camera, the face goes in a circle above the card.
- The title says how the match went, in the clip's language: "นำ 2-0 แต่โดนพลิกแพ้ 2-3" gives a reason to watch;
  "ไฮไลท์นัดนี้" gives none.
- Look: vivid.
- Sounds and effects: none over the crowd and the commentator at the goal, they are the sound of the moment. At most
  one effect, on the ball going in, never over the ball while it is in play.

## Checked at the end

- the captions are words that were said
- the length is inside the Reel limit, and the clip does not start silent or black
