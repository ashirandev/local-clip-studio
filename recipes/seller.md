# Product review or selling live

After the clip, the viewer knows what the product is, the one thing that makes it worth buying, and its price if
the seller said one.

## Finding the moment

- Rank the stretches by what is said: the product named, a number (price, size, capacity, battery), a spec word,
  a before and after.
- Loudness says little here. A person talking at a table is just as loud the whole time.
- Prefer a stretch where the product is in the frame while the seller says what it does.
- In a selling live with many products, each product gets its own clip.

## Rules

- Every price or number on screen is one the seller said, written exactly as it was said. Never round, convert or
  guess a number.
- The product stays in the frame. Keep the seller's face too when it is in view.
- No text covers the product or the price. When you make the clip, name the product box around the product in the
  picture, so the program keeps words off it and checks it stays in the frame.
- Captions are the seller's own words.

## The clip

- Layout: the vertical frame follows the product, and keeps the face when it is in view.
- The title names the product or its one best point, in the clip's language.
- A spec card may show one number the seller said.
- Look: warm, so faces look natural and a white product stays white. One slow push while the product is shown.
- Sounds: a ding when a price or a number title comes on, and nothing more: the voice sells.
- A glass box under a price or a short list when the picture behind is busy, with the words on it. At most one sticker, such as money_bag on the price.

## Checked at the end

- every number on screen was said in that piece
- the product is in the frame
- the captions are words that were said
- the length is inside the Reel limit, and the clip does not start silent or black
